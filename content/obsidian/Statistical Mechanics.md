# Statistical Mechanics

Statistical mechanics studies how microscopic rules generate macroscopic behavior.

A common example is the canonical ensemble, where the probability of a configuration $x$ is

$$
P(x) \propto e^{-\beta E(x)}.
$$

This idea underlies Metropolis sampling, importance sampling, and many simulation workflows.
