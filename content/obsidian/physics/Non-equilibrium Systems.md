# Non-equilibrium Systems

A non-equilibrium system is driven by gradients, forcing, or external baths, so it does not stay at equilibrium.

Examples include:

- diffusion
- drift-dominated flows
- reaction-diffusion systems
- driven lattice models

This note connects to [[Random Walk]], [[Brownian Motion]], and [[Markov Chains]].
