# Brownian Motion

Brownian motion is the continuous-time limit of a random walk.

$$
\mathrm{d}X_t = \sqrt{2D}\,\mathrm{d}W_t
$$

where $D$ is the diffusion coefficient and $W_t$ is a Wiener process.
