# Random Walk

A random walk is one of the simplest models of diffusion and stochastic motion.

$$
S_n = \sum_{k=1}^n X_k
$$

where $X_k$ are independent increments.

This note serves as a simple example of a browser-based simulation page in the labs section and a conceptual note in the Quartz garden.
