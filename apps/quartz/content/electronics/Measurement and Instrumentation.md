# Measurement and Instrumentation

Instrumentation is essential for validating models and translating experiments into data.

Important elements include:

- oscilloscopes
- signal generators
- amplifiers
- grounding and shielding
- calibration routines

This connects naturally to the hardware projects, lab notebooks, and observation-driven research notes.
